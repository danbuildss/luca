How it works

The product

Five things Luca does for your operation.

Watch

Monitors all registered wallets in real time. Base live, Solana coming. No polling on your end.

Classify

Every inbound and outbound transaction gets a label: revenue, expense, gas, treasury transfer, or unknown.

Book

Keeps a running P&L. Cash in, cash out, net position, gas spend — always up to date.

Remember

Learns from corrections. Tell Luca once — that wallet is treasury, that address is a partner — and it stays corrected.

Report

Daily brief via Telegram every morning. On-demand summaries when you ask. Alerts when something needs your attention.

Memory

Luca learns how your operation works.

You
That wasn't an expense. 0x91D belongs to us — it's our treasury wallet.
Luca
Got it.

Reclassified the $3,000 transfer as treasury. Future transfers between these addresses won't be counted as operating expenses.

Correct once. Luca remembers.
