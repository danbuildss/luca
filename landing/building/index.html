Build log

Not everything we shipped. Everything worth remembering.

  1. Sep 27, 2026

    Luca no longer says it changed something it didn't

    Real-wallet testing caught Luca replying "Confirmed" to a request it had not carried out. Luca now only says a change happened after it has been made, and otherwise asks again.

    fixedEvidence

  2. Sep 27, 2026

    The ACCUM fee view can be shared

    The owner of the ACCUM fee wallet can let other Luca users see the fee figures Luca verifies. Nothing else of theirs is shared.

    shippedEvidence

  3. Sep 27, 2026

    Corrections now ask before changing history

    When a correction could apply to earlier transactions, Luca asks before changing those books.

    shipped

  4. Sep 27, 2026

    Books can now be checked against Base

    Luca can verify whether its books match the financial activity onchain.

    shipped

  5. Sep 27, 2026

    ACCUM creator-fee accounting went live

    Luca can now account for the BNKR creator-fee activity used in the ACCUM experiment.

    shipped

  6. Sep 27, 2026

    Incomplete recent-transaction answers fixed

    Luca previously returned a recent-transaction answer that omitted a swap. Real-wallet testing exposed it. The issue was fixed and verified.

    fixed

  7. Sep 27, 2026

    Classification quality reporting fixed

    Old spam-token rules were incorrectly affecting Luca's quality report. They are now excluded.

    fixed

  8. Sep 24, 2026

    Balance reconciliation caught missing gas entries

    Luca found missing network-fee records while checking its books against the chain and repaired them.

    fixed
