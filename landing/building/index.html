Build log

Not everything we shipped. Everything worth remembering.

  1. Oct 6, 2026

    Answer Luca by number

    Luca lists what it could not place as a numbered list. Answering "1 was an expense, 4 and 7 were swaps" used to go to the AI, which wrote its own confirmation and then changed nothing when told yes. Luca now reads a numbered answer itself, matches each number to the transaction it listed, and asks one yes/no question that shows exactly what will change.

    fixedEvidence

  2. Oct 5, 2026

    Money coming in is never learned as an expense

    Real-wallet testing found a rule that labelled every incoming transfer from one address as an expense. A label that runs against the direction of the money now applies to that one transaction only and teaches no rule, and Luca says so when it asks.

    fixedEvidence

  3. Oct 4, 2026

    One morning message, and quiet nights

    Luca now sends one message in the morning, only when something happened, with the whole week on Mondays. Questions about transfers it cannot place go in that message as one numbered list instead of arriving one by one, and nothing is sent between 22:00 and 08:00 in your timezone.

    shippedEvidence

  4. Sep 30, 2026

    Luca recognises staking from the chain

    When tokens move into a staking contract, Luca now reads the contract's own record of the stake before and after the transfer, so a stake is recognised as capital moved, not spending. It found a real 700,000 BNKR stake this way without being told.

    shippedEvidence

  5. Sep 30, 2026

    New wallets: Luca now says when your books are ready

    Our first outside operator asked for his balance seconds after adding a wallet and got an empty answer. Now Luca shows what it can already see, says it is still reading your history, and messages you once your books are ready. Answers that need complete books wait until then. The welcome message is also no longer sent twice.

    shippedEvidence

  6. Sep 28, 2026

    AI classification now runs in production

    Luca's AI classifier, which labels the transfers no rule can, had not been running. It runs now, and anything it can't place stays unknown for you to answer.

    fixedEvidence

  7. Sep 27, 2026

    Luca no longer says it changed something it didn't

    Real-wallet testing caught Luca replying "Confirmed" to a request it had not carried out. Luca now only says a change happened after it has been made, and otherwise asks again.

    fixedEvidence

  8. Sep 27, 2026

    The ACCUM fee view can be shared

    The owner of the ACCUM fee wallet can let other Luca users see the fee figures Luca verifies. Nothing else of theirs is shared.

    shippedEvidence

  9. Sep 27, 2026

    Corrections now ask before changing history

    When a correction could apply to earlier transactions, Luca asks before changing those books.

    shipped

  10. Sep 27, 2026

    Books can now be checked against Base

    Luca can verify whether its books match the financial activity onchain.

    shipped

  11. Sep 27, 2026

    ACCUM creator-fee accounting went live

    Luca can now account for the BNKR creator-fee activity used in the ACCUM experiment.

    shipped

  12. Sep 27, 2026

    Incomplete recent-transaction answers fixed

    Luca previously returned a recent-transaction answer that omitted a swap. Real-wallet testing exposed it. The issue was fixed and verified.

    fixed

  13. Sep 27, 2026

    Classification quality reporting fixed

    Old spam-token rules were incorrectly affecting Luca's quality report. They are now excluded.

    fixed

  14. Sep 24, 2026

    Balance reconciliation caught missing gas entries

    Luca found missing network-fee records while checking its books against the chain and repaired them.

    fixed
